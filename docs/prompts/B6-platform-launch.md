1. `platform.price_plan` (+ versions) per **D-156** (Proposed until the pilot; every value an effective-dated version): **Apartment Starter** THB 25/unit/month, minimum 20 units (A1, A2, A4, resident LIFF, PromptPay); **Apartment Smart** THB 40/unit/month (+A3, C2) with meter hardware sold (~THB 1,500–2,500 per point, invoiced through the getpack order flow) or rented (THB 60/point/month); **Condo Juristic** 15 / 12 / 10 per unit tiered (minimum 100 units) and **Juristic Pro** 20 per unit + B1 6,000–15,000 per building per year; **Access add-on** 3,000–8,000 per building per month; **PM Portfolio** from 50,000/month for 10 buildings; **Landlord** free ≤ 3 units / 99 ≤ 10 / 299 ≤ 20; **Inspection report** 3,000–5,000 per report. Residents, technicians and committee members are free. Plan versions carry the unit price, tiers, minimums, billing unit (`per_unit | per_building | per_tenant | per_report`), term, annual discount (15 %), VAT. 2. `platform.subscription` (tenant, plan version, add-ons, state D-082, trial_ends_at, term dates, discount/pilot terms D-083), `platform.subscription_usage` (monthly snapshot on the 1st: **units counted** = `core.unit` with status ≠ `inactive` in the tenant's buildings, D-156), `platform.platform_invoice` + lines + `platform.platform_payment` + `platform.platform_receipt` + `platform.platform_credit_note` (issued by Collective Group (Thailand) from platform settings; number series with `tenant_id NULL`, D-146; PDF; Thai QR through the platform's own payment adapter), `platform.haas_asset` (device ref, tenant, install date, term, monthly value, state, replacement history, de-install order ref, D-081), `platform.pilot_agreement`, `platform.site_survey_request`, `platform.install_order` (install/de-install/replace per unit for platform field engineers).
3. Lifecycle job: trial → active on first platform payment; past_due after due + grace; suspended = read-only mode enforced by a guard on `/v1/owner|staff` mutations with a clear problem `subscription.suspended` (residents can still view and pay invoices already issued, D-082); cancellation → de-install order.
4. Admin endpoints: plans, subscriptions, invoices, pilot agreements, HaaS register; owner endpoints: current plan, usage, invoices, upgrade Starter → Smart (creates a site-survey request for Field engineers).
5. Acceptance: e2e trial → invoice → paid → active → past_due → suspended (owner mutation blocked, resident payment allowed) → paid → active; unit counting on fixtures (inactive excluded, minimum 20 applied); pilot discount applied; landlord with 3 units billed 0.

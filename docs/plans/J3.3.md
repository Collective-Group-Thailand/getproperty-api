## 0. Shape of the relationship (the conservative reading)
- A `pm_company` tenant **manages** juristic buildings under a **management contract**; the juristic tenant stays the
  building's owner **and operator** (no operator-period switch: the juristic person keeps its books, approvals and
  committee — D-311 / D-320). The PM's window into a building is a **building-scope read grant** (`core.asset_access_grant`,
  reason `pm_contract`, module `juristic` only, valid from the contract start to the day after its end, Bangkok
  midnight) created when the PM accepts, revoked when either side terminates — so access ends with the contract by
  construction, and nothing else of the building (residents, invoices, leases) becomes readable through RLS.
- Portfolio figures are **aggregates** computed per building **in the juristic tenant's context after a live-grant
  check** (the G2 / D-326 pattern): the PM never reads a resident, an invoice or a ledger line, only KPIs.
- The fee is **never paid automatically**: each billing period that has **ended** becomes one *submitted* J2.1
  expense request in the juristic tenant (payee = the PM registered as a juristic vendor, approval bands +
  maker-checker of D-329, payment recorded by the juristic) — idempotent per (contract, period).

## 1. Schema (migration 0081, schema-design §7.5 rows 290–293; §7.6 J3-c refined)
- `juristic.management_contract` (tenant = the juristic; `pm_tenant_id`; building; `vendor_id` (the PM as a vendor
  of the juristic, payee of the fee); title, scope, `fee` + `vat_amount` (numeric(14,2)), `fee_period`
  monthly · quarterly · annual, `due_day` 1–28, `starts_on`, `ends_on`, `expense_account_id`, `wht_key`,
  `resolution_id` (adopted, same building — the meeting appoints the manager), `document_id`, `grant_id`;
  state `draft → offered → active → ended | terminated`, `offered → declined | cancelled`, `draft → cancelled`;
  offered / accepted / declined / cancelled / terminated / ended stamps; version). UQ (building, PM, starts_on);
  **EXCLUDE** two live (offered / active) contracts of one building overlapping in time. RLS **tenant pair**: read and
  update by the juristic **or** the PM tenant, insert by the juristic only, no delete; a trigger refuses any change of
  the terms by the PM tenant (it may only accept / decline / terminate).
- `juristic.management_fee_charge` (tenant = the juristic; contract; `period_key` (`YYYY-MM`, `YYYY-Qn`, `YYYY`),
  period start / end, service days in the term, amount + VAT (prorated by calendar days, half-up), `due_on`
  (= `due_day` of the month after the period), `expense_request_id` UQ; **UQ (contract, period_key)** = the job's
  idempotency). Tenant-pair read (the PM sees what it billed); writes by the juristic only.
- `juristic.pm_staff_assignment` (tenant = the PM; contract; building; `user_id` (an active member of the PM tenant);
  role `building_manager · assistant_manager · accountant · technician · admin_officer · security_supervisor ·
  other`; shift text; `starts_on`, `ends_on` (exclusive, null = open); EXCLUDE same user × building overlapping;
  inside the contract term). RLS: the PM's own rows; the juristic reads the rows of its contracts (who works in its
  building).
- `juristic.expense_request.management_contract_id` (FK, CHECK ⇒ vendor) — the fee request is exempt from the
  ≥ 3-quotation rule (the adopted resolution is the basis, as a renewal resolution is for a vendor contract).

## 2. Juristic side `/v1/owner/juristic/management-contracts` (juristic tenants only)
- `GET` list (state, building, PM) · `POST` draft · `GET {id}` · `PATCH {id}` (draft) · `POST {id}/actions/offer`
  (Idempotency-Key; terms valid, PM is an active `pm_company` tenant, vendor active, expense account active, adopted
  resolution of the building) · `POST {id}/actions/cancel` (draft / offered) · `POST {id}/actions/terminate`
  (Idempotency-Key; reason, effective today … end; grant revoked today or cut at the date) · `GET {id}/charges`
  (with the expense request number / state) · `GET {id}/staff` (who the PM assigned to the building).
- Permissions `juristic.management_contract.read` (owner, manager, accountant, committee, auditor),
  `juristic.management_contract.manage` (owner, manager). Committee / staff limited to buildings
  (`membership.module_scopes.buildingIds`) see only their buildings (404 otherwise).

## 3. PM side `/v1/owner/pm/*` (`pm_company` tenants only; any other tenant type → 404)
- `GET /pm/management-contracts`, `GET …/{id}`, `POST …/{id}/actions/accept` (Idempotency-Key; creates the grant),
  `…/decline` (reason), `…/terminate` (Idempotency-Key; reason, effective date), `GET …/{id}/charges`.
- `GET /pm/portfolio` — the building table: one row per building with an **active contract and a live grant today**:
  juristic name, building, legal form, units, contract (fee, period, end), **collection** (12-month collection rate
  and within-KPI-days rate, last cycle), **arrears** (overdue amount, accounts, > 90 days amount), **budget use**
  (approved budget of today: budgeted vs actual expense, %), **open work** (open service requests, SLA-breached open
  requests, open work orders), **expiring** (active vendor contracts ending within `pm.portfolio_expiring_days`, 90;
  the management contract's own end), staff assigned today; plus totals. `GET /pm/portfolio/buildings/{buildingId}`
  — the same row with the expiring contracts listed.
- Staff: `GET /pm/staff-board` (each active member of the PM tenant with today's assignments; each building with its
  staff; unassigned members), `GET|POST /pm/staff-assignments`, `POST /pm/staff-assignments/{id}/actions/end`.
- Permissions `pm.portfolio.read` (owner, manager, accountant, staff), `pm.contract.read` (owner, manager,
  accountant), `pm.contract.manage` (owner, manager), `pm.staff.read` (owner, manager, staff), `pm.staff.manage`
  (owner, manager). A PM `staff` member sees only the buildings he is assigned to today (and his membership building
  scope if any).

## 4. Job `management-contracts` (hourly, idempotent)
- For each active / ended / terminated contract: every period whose end (capped at the service end) is **before
  today (Bangkok)** and has service days and no charge row → under the contract's row lock, one charge + one expense
  request (draft → submitted; requester = the juristic user who offered the contract, so maker-checker keeps him
  out of the approval) — never paid by the job.
- Active contracts past `ends_on` → `ended` (the grant already expires at the end; revoked for good).

## 2. Co-owner register (`/v1/owner/juristic/register*`)
- `GET …/register?buildingId&q&incomplete` — units with current owners (party, share, since), Σ shares, `incomplete` (Σ < 100), contact channels (phone masked, e-mail, LINE linked, resident login linked), plot fields, ratio, area, bill-to in force.
- `GET …/register/units/{unitId}` — the same plus ownership history, bill-to history, transfers.
- `PATCH …/register/units/{unitId}` — `landAreaSqwa`, `plotNo`, `ownershipRatio`, `areaSqm` (audited, version).
- `POST …/register/units/{unitId}/owners` — add a co-owner (existing `partyId` or a new party), `sharePct`, `validFrom`; Σ current shares ≤ 100 checked in the API (422 `register.shares_exceeded`) and by asset-core's deferred trigger (mapped to the same code).
- `POST …/register/units/{unitId}/transfers` (Idempotency-Key) — whole-unit transfer: every owner current on `transferDate` is closed at that date (exclusive `valid_to`), buyers (existing party or created) open with their shares (Σ ≤ 100); `juristic.ownership_transfer` row; bill-to rows of the sellers end; event `unit_ownership.transferred` (internal, not the inbound `unit.transferred`); audit.
  - **Proration after an issued advance invoice** (`juristic.transfer_proration`, tenant / property, default `carry_to_buyer`): every issued common-fee / fund line of the unit whose period contains the transfer date gets a **credit note** for the days from the transfer date (credit = line − round(line × days before / days), cumulative rounding as J1.1a; reason `ownership_transfer`). `carry_to_buyer` also queues a one-off charge of the same kind to the buyers by share (remainder to the largest share, ties first party id), billed by the next monthly cycle; `refund_seller` credits the seller only (the remainder of the period is not billed to the buyer). Drafts of open cycles are recomputed by the next compute.
- `GET …/register/units/{unitId}/transfers`.

## 3. Bill-to (`juristic.bill_to`, D-307)
- `POST …/register/units/{unitId}/bill-to` — owner party, payer (existing or new party), kind `named_party | agency`, `validFrom`, **written consent document required** (`consentDocumentId`, a document of the tenant) + optional note; one bill-to per owner and day (exclusion constraint); `POST …/bill-to/{billToId}/actions/end`.
- Billing: an owner billable is addressed to the bill-to party in force on the first day of the owner's span in the period; owners naming the same payer are billed on one invoice; their pending one-off charges follow.
- JR: role `payer` with source `bill_to` (no `unit:` scope — a payer sees the invoices addressed to them).

## 4. Charge bases (one engine)
- `ops.rate_basis` + `per_sqwa` (flat price × `land_area_sqwa`) and `budget_split` (budget tiers `{kind: budget, amount, period, splitBy: ownership_ratio | area_sqm | land_area_sqwa | per_unit}`).
- `engine/budget-split.ts` `splitBudget(total, weights)`: each unit half-up of total × weight / Σ weights, the satang remainder to the largest weight (ties: first unit id) → Σ = total exactly. The billing service splits the **period amount** (monthly × months, rounded once) over the building's units; a unit without the weight flags every draft `budget_split_weight_missing` (not issuable).
- Goldens (`test/golden/billing/`): `village-per-sqwa-annual.json` (5 plots of the 248-plot estate, annual), `budget-split-1234567-89.json` (312 ratios Σ exact) + 248-plot total in the spec.

## 5. Budgets & resolutions (§7.6 J1-c … J1-f)
- `juristic.resolution` (draft → adopted → void), `resolution_document`, `juristic.budget` (draft → approved → superseded), `budget_line`.
- CRUD under `/v1/owner/juristic/resolutions*` and `/v1/owner/juristic/budgets*`; adoption (`…/actions/adopt`, Idempotency-Key) and budget approval (`…/actions/approve`, needs an adopted resolution of the building) by `committee` / owner.
- `ops.rate_plan_version.resolution_id`; setting `juristic.rate_requires_resolution` (true): a common-fee / fund version of a juristic building needs an adopted resolution of that building (422 `rate_plan.resolution_required` / `rate_plan.resolution_invalid`).
- `POST /v1/owner/juristic/budgets/{id}/rate-preview` and `…/actions/create-rate-plan-draft`: lines → `budget_split` version (future effective date = editable draft) with per-unit preview (min / median / max, Σ check).

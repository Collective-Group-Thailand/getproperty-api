## 1. Collections ladder (D-305 = D-J04, §7.6 J1-j … J1-m)
- **Templates in code** (`src/modules/ops-juristic/collections/ladder-templates.ts`, like the workflow definitions): one per `core.building_legal_form`. `condo_act` / `land_allocation_act`: remind +3 (LINE, e-mail) · remind +15 · surcharge +30 · notice_letter +45 (letter, LINE, e-mail) · remind +60 · lawyer_letter +90 · suspend_services +180 · legal_action +365; `voluntary` / `lease_contract`: remind +3 · remind +15 · notice_letter +45 · remind +60 (no statutory rule set, gap §6). Offsets are calendar days after the due date of the case's oldest overdue invoice.
- `juristic.collection_ladder` (per tenant × legal form, created from the template the first time a case of that legal form opens or the ladder is read), `juristic.collection_step` {seq, offset_days, action, channels[], template_key, requires_approval, enabled}. Every step but `remind` is **disabled by default**; `suspend_services`, `lawyer_letter`, `legal_action` always require approval (manager / committee / owner, permission `juristic.collections.approve`) — a PATCH may not drop it.
- **Legal gating** (compliance rules `pending_legal_verification`, settings off until O-038): enabling a step, and running it, needs — surcharge: `juristic.surcharge.enabled` + the legal form's `rate_cap`; notice / lawyer letter: `juristic.letters.<legal_form>.template_version` (counsel-approved wording); suspend_services: `juristic.suspend_services.enabled` + the legal form's `min_overdue_days`; legal_action: `juristic.legal_action.enabled`. Enabling otherwise → 422 `collections.legal_review_required`; a step whose gate closed later is recorded `skipped` (reason). The API returns `legalReviewRequired: true` on every step whose action has a pending rule (all but `remind`).
- `juristic.collection_case` per (party, unit) of a juristic tenant, opened by the `invoice.overdue` subscriber (and caught up by the job): anchor = due date of the oldest overdue invoice; state `open | paused | resolved` on the workflow engine (definition `collection_case`: pause / resume / resolve, `workflow.*` events); pause reasons tracked separately (`plan_id`, `dispute_opened_at`): paused while an approved plan is active **or** a dispute is open; days spent paused shift the remaining steps (`paused_days`).
- `juristic.collection_event` append-only timeline (opened, step outcomes `sent | skipped | awaiting_approval | approved | refused | failed`, paused, resumed, dispute opened / closed, resolved) — UQ (case_id, step_id) for step executions ⇒ **each step runs exactly once per case**.
- Job **`collections`** (hourly): opens missing cases, resolves cases with nothing owed (open balance of the party × unit = 0 and no plan charge waiting), runs the next due step of each open case (due = anchor + offset + paused_days; only while something is overdue), in its own transaction per case, idempotent under a row lock. A step needing approval waits (`awaiting_approval`) and blocks the later steps until approved (runs) or refused (skipped, the ladder moves on).
- Pause / resume: `juristic.payment_plan.approved` → pause (plan); `.defaulted` / `.cancelled` / `.completed` → resume (then resolved by the job when nothing is owed); `POST …/collection-cases/{id}/actions/open-dispute` / `close-dispute` (reason, Idempotency-Key) → pause / resume; resident notices `juristic.collection.dispute_opened|closed`.
- **Surcharge** (`collections/surcharge.ts`, pure): per overdue invoice, principal still open (late-fee lines excluded — no compounding) × min(tenant rate, cap) ÷ 100 × days ÷ 365 from max(due date, last surcharge date) to the step date; escalated rate / cap after `escalate_after_days`; half-up per invoice; queued as a `late_fee` charge (source `penalty`) for the next monthly cycle; case `surcharged_through` prevents double counting. Rule `compliance.<legal_form>.surcharge_cap` (pure evaluator) blocks without a cap or above it. `voluntary` / `lease_contract` have no surcharge rule → never surcharged.
- Step effects: remind → resident notification (`juristic.collection.reminder`); notice / lawyer letter → PDF letter (document type `collection_letter`, series CLT, via the document pipeline subscriber) + notification; suspend_services → recorded + resident notice of the common services named in the setting (never utilities or access, `compliance.juristic.no_utility_cutoff`); legal_action → recorded + operator notice.
- **Apartments keep `ops.dunning_reminder`** unchanged; for juristic tenants the dunning subscriber does nothing (no per-invoice reminders, no `late_fee.*` assessment) — the ladder replaces it (rule documented in D-326).

# G5 — Boost & growth billing (plan)

Step G5 of `docs/prompts/G-growth-api.md` (module E6). Requirements: **D-284** (agency + landlord free, unlimited —
nothing here is a subscription or a seat), **D-287** (boost, success / co-broke fee, verified photo / 360°), **D-291**
(every price is an effective-dated price-plan version or a setting, never hard-coded), D-293 (G2 deals and fee
accruals), D-292 (G1 purchase invoices), D-250 (an unpaid purchase never suspends anyone), D-091 (credit notes
maker-checker), ADR-0008 (no funds held for others). Open: **O-027** (every growth price — until a version with a price
is published a product is "not on sale"), **O-020** (gateway — skipped: nothing is charged through it), O-008 (the
platform merchant for the Thai QR of platform invoices), new **O-032** (media partner).

Reuse, never duplicate: platform invoices (`PlatformBillingService.issue`, `purchase` kind without a subscription, line
kinds `boost` / `success_fee` / `co_broke_fee` / `media_service` already in the enum since 0058), price plans and their
admin version / publish endpoints (B6.1), the `productTerms` carry-over of `terms.product` (G1), the G2 accruals
(`ops.fee_accrual`, append-only except `platform_invoice_line_id` / `credit_note_id`), the platform credit-note
maker-checker endpoints (B6.1), `platform_payment.confirmed` (settled) as the activation signal (G1 pattern).

## 2. Products = price plans (seed, no price — O-027)
`boost_listing_7d` (addon, `per_listing`, terms `{product: boost, target: listing, days: 7}`), `boost_listing_30d`
(days 30), `boost_building_month` (`per_building`, `{target: building, months: 1}`), `media_verified_photo` /

## 3. Boosts (`BoostsService`, platform-subscriptions)
- Catalogue: active boost plans whose version in force has a price → `onSale`; building boosts are **free** when the
  setting `boost.building.free` (platform default false, per-tenant override — e.g. Apartment Smart customers, O-027)
  is true, from the plan's newest version terms even without a price.
- Purchase (Idempotency-Key): a published, not hidden listing of the tenant / a building the tenant owns or operates;
  not on sale → 409 `boost.not_on_sale`; same product already waiting for payment on that target → 409
  `boost.pending`; a free building boost while one is in force → 409 `boost.already_active`. Writes the boost
  `pending_payment` + a `purchase` platform invoice (line kind `boost`, snapshot product / target / duration); price 0
  or free → active at once (no invoice).
- Active on payment (`platform_payment.confirmed` settled): window starts now, or at the end of the target's last
  active boost (queued), for `days` × 24 h / `months` calendar months. Job `boosts` (hourly): ended → `expired`
  (nothing else changes). Unpaid = never activated, nothing suspended.
- **Ranking** (`PublicListingsService.search`): up to `boost.search.max_per_page` (setting, default 3, 0 = off)
  boosted listings that match the same filters are put on top of each page, labelled `sponsored: true` +
  `sponsoredLabel` ("โฆษณา" / "Ad"); each boosted listing appears once across pages (page k shows boosted ranks
  k·N … k·N+N−1); ordered by verified-vacant (D-296), then freshness (published_at), then id; organic duplicates of the
  page are dropped; the owner page (`ownerTenantId`) has no sponsored slots. A listing boost or a boost of its
  building counts.

## 4. Success / co-broke fees on the platform invoice (`FeeInvoicingService`)
- Job `growth-fees` (hourly, idempotent, advisory lock per tenant): every agency with accruals of a **past** Bangkok
  month not yet invoiced gets **one** `purchase` invoice (lines `success_fee` / `co_broke_fee`, one per accrual, unit /
  deal in the description); a monthly cap per kind (`terms.monthlyCapAmount` of the fee version that priced the
  month's latest accrual — no backdated version is ever needed) becomes a negative `discount` line; VAT at each version's rate on the net; each accrual's
  `platform_invoice_line_id` is set in the same transaction (never twice — the column is the guard). Snapshot
  `product = growth_fees`, months, accrual ids with the charged net.
- Disputes: admin `POST /v1/admin/fee-accruals/{id}/actions/dispute` (reason) requests a platform credit note for the
  accrual's charged amount incl. VAT on its invoice and links it (`credit_note_id`); approval / rejection through the
  existing maker-checker endpoints; a rejected note may be disputed again; not invoiced yet → 409 `fee.not_invoiced`;
  an open / issued note → 409 `fee.already_disputed`.
- Admin `GET /v1/admin/fee-accruals` (tenant, state uninvoiced / invoiced / disputed / credited).

## 5. Verified photo / 360° orders (`MediaServicesService`)
- Owner: `GET /v1/owner/media-service-products`, `GET/POST /v1/owner/media-service-orders`, `GET …/{orderId}`.
  Order (Idempotency-Key) for a listing of the tenant (unit from the listing): not on sale → 409
  `media_service.not_on_sale`; an open order for the unit and service → 409 `media_service.order_open`; invoice line
  kind `media_service`; `requested` on payment (price 0 at once).
- Platform (partner fulfils, O-032): `GET /v1/admin/media-service-orders[/{id}]`, `POST …/{id}/actions/schedule`
  (`scheduledFor`, `partnerRef`), `POST …/{id}/deliverables` (presign a tenant document — `listing_photo` / `other`,
  unit scope, counted to the tenant's quota, D-286), `POST …/{id}/actions/deliver` (`documentIds`, uploaded
  documents of that tenant and unit), `POST …/{id}/actions/cancel` (reason; money back only by a credit note through
  Finance).

## 6. No stored-value wallet
Every purchase is a platform invoice line (no prepaid credits, no balance) — recorded as a decision (e-money rules,
conservative).

## 1. Chart of accounts (§7.6 J2-a)
- Templates **in code** per `core.building_legal_form` (`src/modules/ops-juristic/accounting/chart-templates.ts`, like the ladder templates): Thai + English names, codes 1xxx assets (cash, petty cash, bank operating, receivables, WHT prepaid, fund bank), 2xxx liabilities (payables, WHT payable, unearned revenue, deposits held, output VAT), 3xxx equity (sinking fund, accumulated surplus), 4xxx income (common fee, fund — not used while funds are equity —, surcharge, other), 5xxx expenses (personnel, utilities, repairs & maintenance, services / vendors, administration, insurance, taxes & fees, petty cash differences). Every template account carries a **role** (`receivable`, `bank`, `unearned`, `income_common_fee` …) the posting rules use; `condo_act` and `land_allocation_act` differ only in names (นิติบุคคลอาคารชุด / หมู่บ้านจัดสรร wording), `voluntary` / `lease_contract` reuse the `condo_act` structure.
- The tenant's chart (`juristic.account`) is copied once from the template of the legal form of its oldest juristic building (fallback `condo_act`) on first use (first posting or first read) and is then its own: owner / manager / accountant add accounts (code unique per tenant, type fixed once used) and rename / deactivate them (system role accounts cannot be deactivated). Fund accounts are created with each fund.

## 2. Journals (§7.6 J2-b, J2-c)
- `juristic.journal` (building, `posted_on`, `source_type`, `source_id`, memo, `reverses_journal_id`, `resolution_id`, `fund_id`) + `juristic.journal_line` (account, debit **or** credit > 0, unit, party, fund). **Balanced in the database**: deferred constraint triggers on both tables check Σ debit = Σ credit and ≥ 2 lines per journal at commit (an unbalanced or empty journal rolls the transaction back). **Immutable**: no UPDATE / DELETE for anyone (trigger), corrections are reversal journals (`source_type = reversal`, UQ per reversed journal). UQ (tenant, source_type, source_id) for every automatic source → every posting is idempotent.
- Posting rules (subscriber `juristic-ledger`, events of juristic tenants only; the same functions back-post):
  - `invoice.issued`: Dr receivable (total) / Cr per line — common fee → income common fee; surcharge (`late_fee`) → income surcharge; **fund** → the building's sinking fund **equity** (D-329: contributions are fund equity, never revenue; O-043); `plan_instalment`, `prior_balance` → receivable (the debt only moved); `deposit_offset` → deposits held; everything else → other income; a line with revenue-schedule rows (advance cycle, D-323) → **unearned revenue** instead of income; VAT → output VAT. Negative lines flip side.
  - revenue recognition (job): per due row (month ≤ this Bangkok month) Dr unearned / Cr income of the line's kind, posted on max(month, issue day); **fills `recognised_at`, `recognised_amount`, `journal_id`**; fund rows are marked recognised without a journal (equity). Credit notes on scheduled lines take the unearned part from the **last** months first (`recognisable = clamp(U − C − prefix, 0, row)`), so Σ recognised = line − credited.
  - `invoice.voided`: reversal of the invoice journal. `credit_note.issued`: Dr the line's income / unearned (unrecognised part first) / fund equity, Cr receivable; reason `payment_plan` → receivable both sides (the debt moved onto the plan — revenue unchanged). `debit_note.issued`: as an invoice.
  - `payment.confirmed` (not `offset`): Dr bank (the GL account linked to the payment's tenant bank account, else the operating bank) or cash (method cash), Dr WHT prepaid (wht withheld by the payer), Cr receivable (amount + WHT; excess = negative receivable = payer credit, like `ops.receivable_entry`). `payment.reversed`: reversal. `payment.refunded`: Dr receivable / Cr bank.
  - expense paid: Dr expense per line, Cr WHT payable, Cr paid-from (bank / cash / fund bank); paid from a fund also Dr fund equity / Cr accumulated surplus (fund utilisation). Petty cash top-up: Dr petty cash / Cr bank. Spend: Dr expense / Cr petty cash. Count difference: Dr petty cash difference expense / Cr petty cash (or reverse). Fund transfer in: Dr fund bank / Cr operating bank; out (resolution): Dr operating bank / Cr fund bank + Dr fund equity / Cr accumulated surplus. Manual journals (opening balances, WHT remittance, corrections) by owner / manager / accountant.
- Job **`juristic-ledger`** (hourly): recognises due revenue rows and back-posts every invoice / note / payment / refund / void of juristic tenants that has no journal yet (chronological; idempotent by the source UQ) — existing data is posted on first run.

## 3. Expenses (§7.6 J2-e, J2-f, J2-h)
- `juristic.expense_request` (+ `expense_request_line` {account, description, amount, vat_amount, wht rate key/rate/tax}, `expense_attachment` {document, kind quotation · invoice · receipt · payment_evidence · other}); kind `expense` | `petty_cash_top_up`; `draft → submitted → approved | rejected → paid`, `cancelled` before paid; number series `EXP`. Totals: amount = Σ lines, WHT = Σ round(base × rate), net payable = amount − WHT.
- **Approval bands** setting `juristic.approval_bands` (platform / tenant, owner edits): ascending `upTo` amounts (last null) → `roles` (owner · manager · committee) + `approvals` (1–5). The band is snapshotted at submit; each approval is a distinct user ≠ requester (maker-checker D-091, **DB trigger** too) holding a band role; enough approvals → `approved`; one rejection → `rejected`. Default bands (editable, assumption): ≤ 10,000 one of owner / manager / committee; ≤ 100,000 two of owner / manager / committee; above, three of owner / committee.
- Pay (`juristic.expense.pay`, owner / manager / accountant; Idempotency-Key): method bank transfer · cheque · cash · fund (from the request's fund), paid on, reference, evidence document (required except cash) → journal + `paid`. Paid from a fund: the request carries the fund and a **resolution** (DB CHECK) of the fund's building.
- **WHT** rates as data: setting `juristic.wht_rates` (key, th / en label, income type, rate %, `verified: false` — ⚖ O-041); a line picks a key (the rate is snapshotted). On payment with WHT > 0: one `juristic.withholding_certificate` per request (series **WHT**, gapless; form ภ.ง.ด.53 for a company payee, ภ.ง.ด.3 for a person; payee tax id required on the party → 422 `wht.payee_tax_id_missing`); PDF (50 ทวิ layout, ⚖ draft notice) built by the worker subscriber (`juristic.withholding_certificate.issued` → document pipeline, kind `pdf_certificate`).

## 4. Petty cash (§7.6 J2-g)
- `juristic.petty_cash` (building, name, custodian user, float amount, state) + `juristic.petty_cash_entry` (top_up · spend · count_difference; amount, date, description, expense account, receipt document — required for spends, journal). Balance = Σ entries; a spend above the balance → 422 `petty_cash.insufficient`. Top-ups are expense requests of kind `petty_cash_top_up` (approval bands apply) — paying one adds a `top_up` entry. **Reconciliation** = a count (`juristic.petty_cash_count`: counted, book balance, difference) → a `count_difference` entry + journal when ≠ 0.

## 5. Funds (§7.6 J2-d)
- `juristic.fund` (building, kind sinking · reserve · other, name, `bank_account_id` → `platform.tenant_bank_account`, own **fund bank** asset account + **fund equity** account created with it). A building's first sinking fund is created automatically when the first fund contribution is posted. Fund ledger = journal lines on its accounts; balance = fund bank, equity.
- `juristic.fund_transfer` (direction in · out, amount, counter account, resolution, note, requested → approved → (posted) · rejected): **out requires `resolution_id` (DB CHECK)** of an adopted resolution of the fund's building, and an approval by another user (`juristic.fund.approve` owner / manager / committee; requester ≠ approver, DB CHECK); approval posts the journal. Expense payments from a fund need a resolution too (DB CHECK on the request).

## 2. getbuild rental handoff (`RentalHandoffsService`, bridge-integrations)
- Inbound consumer of `unit.transferred` (registered with `IntegrationEventsService.onInbound`): `rentalIntent: false`
  → applied, nothing else; true → one handoff (a re-send of the same transfer = no new row), campaign tag from setting
  `getbuild.handoff.campaign_tags` (developer ref → tag) else `getbuild:<developer ref>`, facts from the port.
- Port `GETBUILD_HANDOFF` (`GETBUILD_MODE` off | fake): `unitFacts(...)` (project, address, unit no, area, bedrooms,
  bathrooms, floor) and `deliverInvitation(...)` — getbuild holds the buyer relationship and shows the invitation in
  its buyer portal; getproperty never messages the buyer itself (no consent of its own). Off → nothing delivered,
  the handoff waits; the admin can resend.
- Job `rental-handoffs` (every 5 min): undelivered invited handoffs get a fresh token (sha256 kept) and are delivered
  (port call outside the transaction); invited past `expires_at` (`getbuild.handoff.valid_days` 30) → expired.
- `GET /v1/public/handoffs/{token}` (facts, campaign, state; no buyer data beyond the first name),
  `POST /v1/public/handoffs/{token}/actions/decline`.
- `POST /v1/owner/rental-handoffs/actions/claim` (Idempotency-Key; landlord tenant): `{token, rent, titleTh?}` → the
  unit is added to the landlord's portfolio (ownership source `getbuild_transfer`) and a **prefilled listing draft**
  (title, facts, campaign tag) is created → `accepted`; when that listing is published → `listed`
  (`listing.published` subscriber). `GET /v1/owner/rental-handoffs`.
- Admin: `GET /v1/admin/rental-handoffs`, `POST /v1/admin/rental-handoffs/{id}/actions/resend`.

## 3. Employer housing (`EmployerHousingService`, ops-leases)
- `GET/POST /v1/owner/employer-agreements`, `GET/PATCH …/{id}` (If-Match version), `PUT …/{id}/workers`
  (employee numbers), `PUT /v1/owner/leases/{leaseId}/employer` (attach / detach; only open leases; issued invoices
  never change, D-040).
- Billing: billables, final invoices and charges address `payer_party_id ?? lessee_party_id`; residents of a payer lease
  do not see the employer's invoices in the LIFF (they pay nothing to the landlord).
- `GET …/{id}/statement?month=YYYY-MM`: invoices addressed to the employer, issued in the Bangkok month, for the
  agreement's leases; per invoice the split per occupant (equal, satang remainder to the first) or per unit (lessee),
  each share's payroll deduction = share × `deduct_percent` and the employer's net; totals.
- `POST …/{id}/payroll-exports` (Idempotency-Key, `export.create`) → export job kind `payroll_deduction` (CSV: employee
  no, name, unit, invoice, share, deduction; UTF-8 BOM; formula-injection safe).
- TM30: `GET /v1/owner/tm30-exports?employerAgreementId=` lists the foreign workers' notifications of the agreement's
  leases (the B6.2 rows; the upload file stays per building).

## 4. Relocation network (`RelocationService`, ops-listings)
- Consent purpose `resident.relocation` (resident, optional, th / en, never pre-ticked). Subscriber `lease.notice_given`:
  for each user linked to the lessee / co-lessees **who granted it** → one open offer + LINE `relocation.offer_ready`;
  nobody else gets a row. `consent.changed`: granted later → offers for leases already under notice; withdrawn → open
  offers `withdrawn`, destination erased at once.
- `GET /v1/resident/relocation-offers` (consent re-checked on every read; listings matched only while consented),
  `PUT …/{id}/destination` (area slug or province / district, max rent), `POST …/{id}/actions/enquire`
  (Idempotency-Key; the B7.3 enquiry as a prospect + optional B7.11 rent-record share = the record travels to the new
  landlord only by this action), `POST …/{id}/actions/dismiss`. Offers close when the lease ends (+
  `relocation.offer.keep_days` 30).
- Matching: published public listings (B7 search) in the destination, rent ≤ max rent, not of the leaving landlord's
  tenant, at most `relocation.offer.max_listings` (10). No profile is stored beyond the destination of the offer.

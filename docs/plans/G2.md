## 3. Code
- `AgencySignup` = `LandlordSignupService` generalised (`type: landlord | agency`), `POST /v1/public/signup/agency`
  (+ `/otp`, same OTP purpose as landlord sign-up).
- Tenant-type lists learn `agency` (menu, `TenantTypeName`, storage settings, admin onboarding enum, price-plan seed).
  Menu: `listings`, `leads` for landlord + agency; new `mandates`, `deals` (landlord + agency), `mandated_units`,
  `co_broke` (agency).
- `MandatesService` (`src/modules/ops-listings/mandates.service.ts`): invite (owner; unit it holds or whose building it
  owns/operates; grantee must be an active `agency` tenant; exclusive conflict 409), accept (agency; creates the
  grant through RLS as the owner, state `active` when `valid_from` ≤ today else `accepted`), decline (agency →
  revoked), revoke (owner), end (either party) — revocation revokes the grant and closes the agency's live listings
  of the unit in the same transaction; daily job `mandates-maintenance` activates / ends by date. Audit + events
  `mandate.invited|accepted|activated|ended|revoked`.
- Listings: an agency drafts a listing for a mandated unit (`mandate_id` set; facts read as platform after the mandate
  check because a unit grant does not show the building); owner verification is the owner's; listing rules of B7 stay
  (one live listing per unit).
- `CoBrokeService`: propose (listing agency, on its listing), sign (co-agency), cancel (either before signing), end.
- `DealsService` subscriber on `lease.activated` and `payment.confirmed`: lease of a unit with a listing / mandate,
  activated + e-signed (lease_signature) + a confirmed payment on one of its invoices → `ops.deal` once (UQ lease_id,
  `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`), `deal.closed`; accruals per published price-plan version (`success_fee` → listing agency,
  `co_broke_fee` → co-agency; `percent_of_amount` of the recorded commission or monthly rent per `terms.basis`, or
  `per_deal`; `terms.capAmount`); no price → no accrual (not sold). Seeds: plans `success_fee`, `co_broke_fee`
  (tenant type agency, draft, no price).
- Endpoints (owner surface, permissions reused: owner side `tenant.grant.manage`, agency side `listing.manage` /
  `listing.read`): `GET /v1/owner/agencies?q=`, `GET/POST /v1/owner/mandates`, `GET /v1/owner/mandates/{id}`,
  `POST …/{id}/actions/accept|decline|revoke|end`, `GET /v1/owner/mandated-units`,
  `GET/POST /v1/owner/co-broke-agreements`, `GET …/{id}`, `POST …/{id}/actions/sign|cancel|end`,
  `GET /v1/owner/deals`, `GET /v1/owner/deals/{id}` (with accruals for the payer).
